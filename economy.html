In-game money

How prices move

Earn coins by playing — farming, selling, trading. Java and Bedrock share the same wallet. The shop is not stuck on one number. It follows what people do.

  • Earn Sell to the shop or to friends. Play survival.
  • Spend Buy from the shop. Pay other players.
  • Prices move If everyone sells cobble, cobble pays less.

You pay

Buy from shop

What it costs to take an item out. If lots of people keep buying the same thing, this number often goes up.

You get paid

Sell to shop

What the shop pays you. If everyone dumps the same item, this number often goes down.

Easy picture

A lemonade stand

If everyone wants lemonade, the stand raises the price a little. That’s demand.

If everyone keeps bringing lemons, they pay less for more. That’s supply.

The shop does the same idea in the background, so one farm can’t stay easy money forever.

Deeper bit

The city is the signal

The shop watches how much moved, and whether people were mostly buying or selling. Demand-heavy → prices tend to rise. Supply-heavy → prices tend to fall. It’s feedback from how you play — not a random spin.
