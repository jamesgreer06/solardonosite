How we run the city

Hosting costs land around $50–$60 per month. Donations and the goal bar go straight toward that — not ranks, crates, or gameplay boosts. Surplus when we’re ahead carries to the next bill so the math stays easy to follow.

Vanilla-first. Mining, building, and exploring stay the point. Plugins cover economy, crossplay, and quality-of-life (homes, warps, messaging) — enough to trade and travel together, not enough to replace the survival game underneath.

Funding

Donations are set up on Discord so we can match your Minecraft username to the supporter wall.

Crossplay

Bedrock connects through Geyser — you share the same survival world, economy, and warps as Java, not a separate lobby.

The town square

Announcements, votes, and “is the End open?” live on Discord. If the site is the front door, Discord is the city inside.

Rules

Three principles — staff apply them with context, not robot bans. Written so kids and grown-ups can both follow them.

  1. No hacking or cheating — including x-ray, fly hacks, auto-clickers meant to skip human play, or anything that copies unfair client powers.
  2. No unfair gameplay advantages — if it gives you an edge others can’t reasonably match, ask staff before using it.
  3. Respect the community — be kind in chat. Harassment, hate, or sustained meanness can still get you removed even if it isn’t “technical cheating.”

Mods & clients: Quality-of-life (for example zoom) is usually fine. Anything that reveals hidden ores, entities, or automates combat/building beyond fair human play is not. See the prohibited mods list for Java-only mods we ban so Bedrock players stay on fair ground.

Enforcement: Staff may warn, mute, kick, or ban depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before. If you are not sure something is allowed, ask on Discord before you rely on it.

Economy

Money is earned in-world — farming, selling, player trade — and spent at the server shop or with each other. Nothing here is bought with real money. Java and Bedrock use the same wallet.

Balances & trade

Pay players, check your balance, and toggle who can pay you — useful when you want privacy from tips or spam.

Shop & selling

Buy from the server shop; sell through the sell GUI or bulk — prices shift over time, not fixed forever.

Supply & demand pricing

Dynamic pricing: lots of selling to the shop pushes sell payouts down; lots of buying pulls buy prices up. When activity balances, prices settle more.

Auction house
