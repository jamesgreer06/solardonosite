Keep it kind

Rules

Three easy rules. Staff help if something goes wrong.

  1. No cheating — no x-ray, fly hacks, or bots that play for you.
  2. No unfair tricks — if it gives you a secret edge, ask first.
  3. Be kind — no bullying, hate, or mean spam in chat.

Some computer mods are not allowed, so phone and console players stay even. See the mod list or ask on Discord.
